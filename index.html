❤️ How to Play

1. Play regular UNO with your usual rules.
2. After playing a card, tap Reveal Activity.
3. Complete the revealed challenge together, if both agree.
4. Take turns and enjoy the surprises.
5. Use Skip whenever you like, with no penalty.

Optional UNO twists:
🔴 Red: A kissing challenge.
🔵 Blue: A relaxing massage.
🟢 Green: A sensory experience.
🟡 Yellow: A playful couple's challenge.
🃏 Wild: Choose a challenge together.
+2: Add a little extra time to a mutually chosen activity.
Reverse: Switch who gives and receives.
Skip: Choose to pass on the activity.
+4: Pick from four activities, then agree on one.

These twists are optional house rules. You can adapt them to match the UNO cards you play.
